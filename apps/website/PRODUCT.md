# Navori

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Developers evaluating AI coding tools, and nontechnical recruiters or team advocates who need to understand the potential well enough to introduce Navori to their teams.

## Product Purpose

Navori configures how coding assistants work inside a project: project context, specialist roles, tools, permissions, verification and optional persistent memory. It generates engine-specific resources from versioned configuration.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The site uses Astro and supports English, Spanish and Simplified Chinese. Engine capabilities differ; the landing must not imply identical enforcement across hosts. Plugins are optional. Examples are illustrative, not measured results or real customer conversations.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the current Navori identity and restore a more visible square background grid. Explain the product in basic language before introducing technical terminology.

## Evidence on Hand

CLI commands, supported engines, inventory and release notes are maintained in this repository. No customer outcomes or productivity benchmarks were supplied.

## Product Principles

- Explain the team's benefit before listing implementation details.
- Show a concrete task progressing through the workflow.
- Keep technical depth available without making it the main reading path.
- Do not promise that configuration eliminates human review.
